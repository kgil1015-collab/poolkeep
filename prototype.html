Shock & Acid Guide
Calculated doses with timing, safe handling, and storage rules.
🚫
Critical Storage Warning
NEVER store chlorine shock and muriatic acid near each other. They react to produce toxic chlorine gas. Keep in separate, ventilated locations — minimum 10 feet apart. Never reuse containers or mix leftover chemicals.
☁️
Pool Shock
Eliminates chloramines · restores water clarity

Timing & Method
⏰ Always shock at dusk or night — UV destroys chlorine rapidly
💧 Pre-dissolve granular shock in a bucket of pool water first
🔄 Run pump continuously for at least 8 hours
⏳ Wait until FC drops to 4–6 ppm before swimming (24–48 hours)
🧪 Retest water within 24 hours
🧤
Safe Handling — Shock
Wear chemical-resistant gloves and splash-proof goggles. Never mix different shock brands or types. Never add to skimmer. Keep away from heat, flame, and direct sunlight. Store in original container in a cool, dry place.
🧪
Muriatic Acid
Lowers pH and total alkalinity

Timing & Method
⏰ Add at dusk — acid reacts faster in warmer sun-heated pools
💧 Pour slowly around the perimeter while walking — never dump in one spot
🔄 Keep pump running for at least 4 hours after adding
⏳ Wait 4 hours minimum before swimming
🧪 Retest pH after 4–6 hours
🧤
Safe Handling — Muriatic Acid
Wear chemical-resistant gloves and eye protection. Always add acid TO water — never water to acid. Work outdoors in a well-ventilated area — fumes are toxic. Keep away from metals and clothing. Store upright in original container, cool and dry, far from all chlorine products.
